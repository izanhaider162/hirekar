Questions, answered straight

Do I need an account to order? +

No. HireKar V1 has no logins at all. You pick a gig, confirm on WhatsApp, pay via JazzCash or Easypaisa, and receive your work. That's it.

When do I pay — before or after the work? +

You confirm the full brief on WhatsApp first. Payment is made once the order is confirmed and before the seller starts, via JazzCash or Easypaisa. Packages include free revisions until you approve the final files, and we stay in the chat until delivery.

Is there escrow or buyer protection? +

Not in V1 — we're honest about that. There is no escrow system yet. What you do get: vetted sellers, a written brief confirmed before payment, included revisions, and our team in the WhatsApp conversation. Escrow and in-site payments are planned for V2.

How fast will I get my work? +

Each gig shows its delivery time — most photo and video gigs deliver in 1–2 days, websites in 4–5 days. Need it faster? Ask on WhatsApp before ordering and the seller will tell you honestly if it's possible.

Can I sell my services on HireKar? +

Yes — if you edit video, build websites or edit photos. Send your portfolio on WhatsApp using the “Apply as a seller” button above. Our team reviews every seller before a gig goes live.

What if I don't like the delivery? +

Use the revisions included in your package and tell the seller exactly what to change — be specific, it helps. If a delivery doesn't match the confirmed brief at all, message us on the same WhatsApp thread and we'll sort it out with the seller.
