Common questions

Do I need an account to order? +

No — you can order on WhatsApp without one. A free buyer account saves each order in your dashboard with its status. Sellers need an account to publish gigs.

When do I pay? +

After the brief is confirmed on WhatsApp, before the seller starts — via JazzCash or Easypaisa. Revisions in your package are free until you approve the final files.

Is there escrow? +

Not yet. Payment is manual, with no escrow or automatic payment system. You get a written brief before paying, included revisions, and order tracking in your dashboard.

How fast is delivery? +

Most photo and video gigs take 1–2 days, websites 4–5. Need it sooner? Ask on WhatsApp before ordering.

Can I sell on HireKar? +

Yes, if you edit video, build websites or edit photos. Sign up free as a Seller and publish from your dashboard.

What if I don't like the delivery? +

Use your package's revisions and say exactly what to change. If it doesn't match the brief at all, message us on the same WhatsApp thread.
